
###Types of Sub-Labels  

 

####<title>   

Name of the legislative text, decision, or publication (full or shortened form, including aliases, abbreviations, and other contextual naming). 

 

Examples: 

-Bell Canada v. Canada (Attorney General)  

-Dunsmuir v. New Brunswick 

-Standards of Review in Administrative Law 

-Statelessness in Canadian Context: A Discussion Paper 

-Immigration and Refugee Protection Act 

-Constitution Act, 1867 

-Ontario Human Rights Code 

-Charter (when used a contextual alias in the document)  

-CCQ (when used as a contextual abbreviation in the document)  

-Dunsmuir (when use as a contextual alias for Dunsmuir v. New Brunswick in the document)  

-The Act (when used as contextual naming of a specific legislation referenced in the document)  

 

--- 

 

####<citation>   

Citation details for legislation or decisions (year, reporter, chapter, court, etc.).  

 

For decisions, each neutral or reporter citation must be wrapped in its own <citation> sub- label. 

 

Examples: 

-2019 SCC 66- 

-R.S.C. 1985, c. C-29- 

- [1999] O.J. No. 2467 (QL)  

-505 U.S. 833 (1992) 

-S.O. 196162, c. 93 

-R.S.O. 1960, c. 71 

 

--- 

 

#### <authors>   

Author(s) of secondary sources. Include all listed authors within a single <authors> sub-label. 

 

Examples: 

-J.L. Baudouin 

-CohenEliya, Moshe, and Iddo Porat 

-C. Massé, J. Baker, C. Bastien et al.  

-Dicey 

 

#### <source>   

 

Publication details for secondary sources (journal, publisher, year, volume, etc.). 

 

Examples: 

- (8th ed. 2014 

- (1983), 43 R. du B. 277 

-10th ed. (1959)  

-Barreau du Québec, vol. 243, Développements récents en droit des assurances (2006), 121 

-38 McGill L.J. 147 

 

####<fragment> 

 

Specific pinpoint reference to a portion of the legal source.  

 

Each fragment must be wrapped in its own <fragment> sub- label. 

 

Examples: 

-section 7 

- s. 7(1)(a)  

- article 1457 

-Part II 

- Schedule I 

-ss. 3–5 

-article 17 et seq 

-para 25 

-paras 26–30 

-p. 353 

-at para 42 

-pp. 225–227 

-no. 125 

-§ 32
